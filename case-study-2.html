CASE STUDY 02

NextGen Agrifarm

Website Design + Front-End Development + Content Architecture

PROJECT CONTEXT

A practical website concept for modern farming education.

NextGen Agrifarm was developed as a self-directed practice project for an agricultural education and consulting concept focused on poultry, hydroponics and training for Kenyan farmers and agripreneurs.

Important: the website was not publicly deployed. It was built for personal practice and portfolio evidence.

DELIVERABLES

What the project demonstrates

Site structure

Home, Poultry, Hydroponics, About Us and Contact sections with clear navigation.

Educational content

Structured farming guides and blog-style content for layers and hydroponics.

Responsive UI

Card layouts, navigation, hero sections and content blocks designed for different screen sizes.

Interaction

JavaScript-driven interface elements including theme persistence, progress indicators, sliders and calculators.

SKILLS EVIDENCE

From content to interface

The project demonstrates that front-end work can be connected to business and content thinking: information is organized first, then translated into usable pages and interactions.
