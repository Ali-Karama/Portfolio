CASE STUDY 03

Busbul Trading Company

Commercial Digital Structure + Product Organization + Catalogue UX

PROJECT CONTEXT

Turning a wholesale business model into a clearer digital experience.

Busbul Trading Company was developed as a self-directed commercial practice project. The work focused on understanding a wholesale business, organizing its product information and translating that structure into a customer-friendly website experience.

Important: this is presented as practice/portfolio work, not as a claimed public client launch.

DIGITAL STRUCTURE

What I designed

Business understanding

Positioned the company around wholesale food and personal-care supply, reliability, pricing and scheduled delivery.

Product organization

Structured products into categories such as flour, rice, salt, sugar, cooking oil, homecare and personal care.

Discovery

Created category navigation, search/filter concepts and product-focused page structures.

Decision support

Included size selection, price display and product comparison concepts to make browsing more useful.

WHY IT MATTERS

Commercial thinking + digital execution.

This case study adds a commercial-business dimension to the portfolio. It shows the ability to move from business information to product taxonomy, customer information and digital interface structure.
